About the Company

Engineering Partners for Industry

SNC Engineering and Contractors is an industrial engineering and contracting company based in the APIIC Industrial Park, Parawada, Visakhapatnam. We work across the full lifecycle of industrial infrastructure — manufacturing equipment, executing structural and piping works, applying protective treatments, and keeping plants running through dependable maintenance support.

In-House Manufacturing

Fabrication of industrial equipment, cable trays, and gratings strictly to client specification.

Site Execution

Structural erection, piping networks, and installation executed under expert supervision.

Protection & Care

Critical thermal insulation and ongoing plant maintenance services to prolong asset lifespan.

Our Services

We provide full-spectrum engineering capabilities from fabrication through to site integration and maintenance.

01

Equipment Manufacturing

Fabrication of industrial process equipment, reaction vessels, storage tanks, and specialty steel items built to exact designs and coordinating parameters.

02

Structural Steel Works

Precision fabrication and structural erection for heavy industrial buildings, pipe racks, technical walkways, support columns, and access platforms.

03

Piping Systems

Process and utility piping fabrication and site routing. Engineered for accurate pressure metrics, leak-proof performance, and chemical compatibility.

04

Thermal Insulation

High-grade protective insulation application for heat retention or dissipation prevention, shielding hot/cold process lines and minimizing plant energy waste.

05

Cable Trays

In-house manufacturing of standard and heavy-duty galvanized cable trays for safe, organized, and code-compliant electrical distribution routing.

06

Steel Gratings

High-load bearing steel gratings manufactured for drainage covers, walkway panels, and structural platform floors, optimized for harsh environments.

07

Plant Maintenance

Dependable maintenance services, equipment shut-down overhauls, pipeline repairs, and structural reinforcements to minimize operations downtime.
